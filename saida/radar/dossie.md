Grand Theft Auto VI launches tomorrow, November 19
The latest installment in the Grand Theft Auto series heads to the state of Leonida, home to the neon-soaked streets of Vice City and beyond.
Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. But when an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida — forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive.
Grand Theft Auto VI arrives on PlayStation 5 Thursday, November 19, available at PlayStation Store.
When the sun fades and the neon glows, everyone has something to gain — and more to lose
The game is set in the state of Leonida, home to the neon-soaked streets of Vice City and beyond in the biggest, most immersive evolution of the series yet.
Jason grew up around grifters and crooks. After a stint in the Army trying to shake off his troubled teens, he found himself in the Keys doing what he knows best, working for local drug runners. Meeting Lucia could be the best or worst thing to ever happen to him. Jason knows how he’d like it to turn out but right now, it’s hard to tell.
Lucia’s father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk, and life has been coming at her swinging ever since. Fighting for her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary. Sheer luck got her out but now more than anything, Lucia wants the good life her mom has dreamed of since their days in Liberty City. Instead of half-baked fantasies, Lucia is prepared to take matters into her own hands.
Leonida is made up of six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga. Here’s a closer look at what makes each region unique.
The glamour, hustle, and greed of America captured in a single city. Each neighborhood has something to offer, from the pastel art deco hotels and bright white sands of Ocean Beach, to the bustling panaderías of Little Cuba and the bootleg brands of the Tisha-Wocka flea market, out to the VC Port, the cruise ship capital of the world. We’re a long way from the ’80s, but Vice City is still the sun and fun capital of America
Life in this tropical archipelago isn’t flashy but it’s easy. Get your buzz on and pull up a deck chair but look out — you are right on the doorstep of some of the most beautiful and dangerous waters in all of America.
You never know what lies beneath the surface of this primordial expanse. The gators may be the most famous attraction, but there are far deadlier predators and weirder discoveries among the mangroves.
Cheap motels, shut-down attractions, and empty strip malls won’t bring the tourists back, but there’s a new economy in this once-popular vacation spot. It’s fueled by malt liquor, painkillers, and truck stop energy drinks.
In the heart of Leonida, American industry and old school values still reign supreme — whatever the cost. The Allied Crystal sugar refinery provides the jobs, while the local biker gang provides almost everything else.
A national landmark up against the state’s northern border, Mount Kalaga offers prime hunting, fishing, and off-road trails. In the lush surrounding backwoods, hillbilly mystics and paranoid radicals live far from the prying eyes of the government.
Spanning stations including Classic Rock, Modern Hip-Hop, Pop, Metal, Dance, Latin, ‘80s, Electronic, Blues, Ballads, Talk Radio, and much more, Grand Theft Auto VI features the biggest and widest-ranging selection of in-game radio yet.
Plus, for the first time ever, Grand Theft Auto VI introduces a series of on-demand podcasts for Jason and Lucia to experience beyond Leonida’s terrestrial radio, with both radio and podcasts available to listen to in vehicles and on foot through newly introduced headphones and earbuds connected to their smartphones.

GTA 6 Gets Officially Licensed Trading Cards Based on In-Game Collectibles
The hunt for GTA 6 trading cards is leaving the virtual confines of Leonida — Rockstar is teaming up with Panini America to bring the in-game collectibles to life.
The officially licensed GTA 6: Sports Trading Card Collection is based on Panini’s standard sports card system, featuring rare-numbered cards and team color parallels. The GTA collection adds Vice City-specific Silver, Pink, Teal, and Light Blue parallels to the standard slate of Legend Bronze, Silver, and Gold.
The collectible company has revealed images of several cards planned for the collaboration, which is set to launch this December.
The Panini Group is an Italian-based collectible company that’s become quite the household name for sports card fans. Panini America is the official collectible partner for leagues like the WNBA, NASCAR, and FIFA, just to name a few.
GTA 6 cards will be released in four-pack boxes, each of which, on average, come with two Rookies, one Retro, one Illustrated, and four Special Silvers. There’s no word on pricing, but hobby boxes currently listed on Panini’s site start at $30 and go up to over $700.
Panini’s card images, shown above, feature a collection of fictional athletes from Vice City’s sports teams. Not all of the featured athletes are entirely fake — for instance, Tyreek Hill, ex-wide receiver for the Miami Dolphins, serves as the voice and likeness for the Vice City Manatees’ Maleek Rivers.
GTA 6 features a total of 480 trading cards across 16 individual sets, and we don't know exactly how many Panini is planning to turn into real cardboard. The sports-focused collectible company is less likely to adapt the collectible cards featuring other GTA 6 characters like Mandy and Vicki, but social media users can hope.

Performance analysis reveals GTA 6 has a more stable frame-rate on Xbox Series X compared to PlayStation 5
If you don't own a PlayStation 5 Pro, the best way to play Grand Theft Auto 6 is reportedly on Xbox Series X. That's according to new performance analysis, which shows Microsoft's console is more consistent at maintaining the game's 30fps frame-rate target than the base PS5.
The findings come from the tech experts Digital Foundry, who published a big report covering all the technical similarities and differences between Sony and Microsoft's consoles.
Unsurprisingly, PS5 Pro is in the lead with no competition, as Xbox elected not to release a mid-generation upgrade for the Series console family. Sony's more powerful console delivers "the definitive console GTA 6 experience," according to Digital Foundry. The PS5 Pro has the most stable 30fps performance of all four consoles, and benefits from a sharper presentation at 4K thanks to Sony's PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) image upscaler, which upscales the image from a 1440p base resolution.
It's when comparing the base consoles - Xbox Series X and PS5 - that things get a little more interesting. Both target a 30fps frame-rate and often maintain that target, but certain sections trigger drops. According to the report, those include sequences with busy crowds and fast vehicle traversal. In some cases, the drops can be as low as mid-to-high 20fps.
With that in mind, PS5 has "substantially more frequent" frame-rate drops compared to Series X. The Xbox console is also aided by its support for 120Hz Variable Refresh Rate (VRR), which helps smooth out small dips in the frame-rate.
"Ultimately, Series X is meaningfully better off," Digital Foundry concluded.
Despite the studio's close marketing alignment with PlayStation historically - to the point Xbox can't even say GTA 6 in certain posts about GTA 6 - this is not the first time a Rockstar game has run better or looked better on an Xbox console.
At Red Dead Redemption 2's launch in 2018, Digital Foundry hailed Xbox One X as the best place to play it, owing to - unsurprisingly - a more stable 30fps frame-rate and sharper visuals. Not much has changed in the past eight years on that front, then.
Given all the information we have today, including the lack of 60fps and 40fps modes on PS5 Pro, it's going to be really interesting to see the kind of PC specs required to achieve parity with console visuals at 60fps and beyond. If Rockstar doesn't get there first, modders are already working on porting GTA 6 to PC.

It looks like Xbox Series X can maintain 30fps more reliably than PS5
Grand Theft Auto 6 gameplay analysis shows that Xbox Series X can maintain 30fps more reliably than the base PS5.
This comes from tests conducted by Digital Foundry, which has compared the performance on the base PS5 directly with Xbox Series X.
While finding that the consoles support the same resolution, it was noted that Xbox experiences fewer framerate dips, and even supports higher-quality visuals in some scenes.
“Like in most other current-gen games, Series X and PS5 look about the same. Key visual settings are matched here,” says Digital Foundry.
“In typical play, both of them hit 30 FPS or don’t fall far below, but I think GTA 6 is balanced on a knife’s edge here in performance terms, and the game can dip below 30 FPS more often than I’d like.
“PS5 seems substantially worse off in this respect,” the analysis continues. “I managed to roughly match a few sequences, and PS5 likes to drop frames more often than Series X in spots that seem to cause issues for this game, like in fast vehicle traversal.”
Digital Foundry argues this is because the visuals are matched, for the most part, across platforms, which hurts PS5 because the Series X is better equipped to handle this level of detail.
However, another point in Xbox’s favour is that there are “minor resolution differences that favour Series X” in some parts of the game.
Despite this, both versions do experience performance dips, so the Series X is not immune to framerate drops.
While stressing that these differences are minor, it comes after Rockstar heavily promoted the PS5 version of the game over the Xbox Series X/S release. This is because Rockstar signed a marketing agreement with Sony, allowing it to say that its console was the best way to experience GTA 6.
This agreement also saw Rockstar promote features that were unique to the PS5 version of GTA 6. These are mostly found in the PS5’s DualSense controller, with haptic feedback used heavily throughout GTA 6.
