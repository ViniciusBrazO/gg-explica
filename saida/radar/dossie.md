GTA 6: How The Ultimate Edition Content Works
Ever since pre-orders for GTA 6 went live and its $100 Ultimate Edition was revealed, one question has been asked more than any other: Is it worth it?
Well, if our poll ran at the time is any indication, 70% of you certainly felt that way, with the majority opting for the full GTA 6 experience over the standard version.
We’ve long known what is included in the more expensive edition, from personalised vehicles and weapons to exclusive stores and missions. But what we didn’t know was how exactly we’d access this extra stuff or when we’d be able to use it. Well, now we do, thanks to the details listed in the in-game store, which states, “Ultimate Edition bonuses are threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia’s Story, with new items uncovered behind each chapter”.
It turns out that these extras will be sprinkled throughout the story, unlocking over the course of its chapters, with some only being available to use during certain timeframes, and even permanently removing your access to them once you pass points in Jason and Lucia’s tale.
Here’s the full list of the bonuses and when you’ll be able to get your hands on each:
Jason’s Vehicles – An army fatigue-tinged Dinka Enduro motorcycle and Crest Kayak. Only available from Jason’s house for use during Chapter 1.
Ganado Retro Build – Jason’s Vapid Ganado low-riding pickup with exclusive mods. Apply at the Coastal Auto Care mod shop in Key Lento. Only available to use in Chapter 1.
Personalised Weapon Variants – Sidearms with detailed engravings for Jason’s Girardi ES9 and Lucia’s Klose K17 pistols. Found in the variants section of the weapon locker at Ammu-Nation. Jason’s is available from Chapter 1. Lucia’s is available in Chapter 2.
Morgan Revolvers – His and hers versions of the revolver with classic Vice City stylings, sourced from the Vercetti Estate. Available from any Ammu-Nation weapon locker in Chapter 2.
One-Eyed Willie’s – A mod shop in Lake Leonida specialising in off-road modifications and hand-painted art. Available from Chapter 1.
Dominator Buggy & Garage – An off-road buggy built for the terrain of Mount Kalaga and its surrounding areas, which can be stored in the Paradise Garage in Watson Bay. Available after visiting the Mud Club with Wyman in Chapter 2.
Classic Car Collection – A string of side objectives that task you with tracking down abandoned classic cars for Wyman to restore them to their former glory. Wyman will contact you on WhatUp once you’ve collected the Dominator in Chapter 2.
Rideout Customs – A mod shop exclusive to the Ultimate Edition that specialises in detailed interiors, rims, and donk stylings. Available from Chapter 1.
PTT Youngin$ Compound – A gang compound you can raid in the Southside area of Vice City, full of special items and distinct contraband. Available from Chapter 1 once you’ve explored Southside.
Stock 305 Clothing Store – A clothing store in the Stockyard area of Vice City with elevated streetwear styles available for Jason and Lucia. Available from Chapter 1.
Sara’s Unisex Salon – A hair salon in Hamlet with signature styles for both Jason and Lucia, including facial hair for Jason and makeup and nails for Lucia. Available from Chapter 1. Signature looks available from Chapter 2.
Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor – Located in Stockyard with over 50 signature tattoo styles for both Jason and Lucia, all designed by the artist collective FAILE. Available from Chapter 1.
Vice City Merch – A collection of apparel inspired by the in-game TV show character, Macca the Gator. Available from the mannequins at The Wonder Whale in Key Lento.
Vice City Style – Exclusive outfits, tattoos, and more for Jason and Lucia. Available in your wardrobe, most tattoo parlors, and at Sara’s Unisex Salon from Chapter 4.
Shitzu Squalo – A gradient pink and blue boat equipped with an explosives-laden weapons crate. Available from the Washington Beach canal dock in Chapter 4.
‘95 Grotti Cheetah – A signature sports car with a white and red retro-futuristic livery. Available from the Pegassi Residences building in Chapter 5.
So, enjoy those early Jason vehicles while you can, as once they’re gone, they’re gone forever. Most of the bonuses are available from early on, though, and remain there for you to use throughout the whole of your adventure through Leonida. Just one word of advice: even though you can visit some places, such as Stock 305 clothing, you may not have the cash early on to actually afford anything there. It’s a pricey place.
