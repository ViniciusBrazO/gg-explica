Grand Theft Auto 6 has the longest story ever seen in a GTA game, which also means that it has an enormous cast of characters. Between rival faction leaders, radio station DJs, and fictional rappers, you're going to meet a whole lot of people in Leonida. And some of their voices might sound very familiar.
While the full cast list is so large that it takes up a good chunk of GTA 6's hour-plus end credits, these are some of the biggest players in the story, including Jason and Lucia. Some you might recognize from movies or even previous Rockstar games, while others have some modest TV credits to their name.
Below, we've listed all the actors who play main roles in GTA 6, along with their previous work that they've starred in.
Much of GTA 6's cast comes from a TV background, and that includes Jason Duval. His voice actor, Dylan Rourke, has appeared on shows like 9-1-1: Lone Star, Station 19, and Grey's Anatomy. GTA 6 is his most high-profile role by a mile, though, unless you count his gig as ”Big Guy” on Modern Family as a star turn.
Manni L. Perez, the actress behind Lucia Caminos, comes from a similar acting background as Dylan Rourke. Perez has popped up in Law & Order: Special Victims Unit, Chicago P.D., and The Blacklist. She's also a Rockstar alumni; Perez voices a Clubber in Grand Theft Auto 5, and a Blackjack Dealer in GTA Online.
Jason and Lucia's comedic sidekick is one of the biggest stars in the entire cast. He's voiced by Bobby Moynihan, who was a principal cast member of Saturday Night Live from 2008 to 2017. You may remember him as playing Drunk Uncle, a recurring character on Weekend Update that already felt like a GTA bit. Moynihan has a long credits list outside of that, most recently appearing in NCIS: Origins, The Fall and Rise of Reggie Dinkins, and Minions & Monsters.
The worst kept secret in all of gaming, Stephen Root plays Brian Heder, Jason's employer. He needs little introduction. Root is the man behind Milton in Office Space, Monroe Fuches in Barry, and Bill in King of the Hill, to name a few of his roles. Funny enough, Root is also credited as Florida Man on the TV series It's Florida, Man. Fitting, no?
A standout in the cast, Dre'Quan plays a major supporting role in GTA 6 once the story gets to Vice City. He's played by Naiqui Macabroad, an actor who has appeared on Law & Order, Chicago P.D., and Crashing. Macabroad spends a lot of time in the story acting alongside of Hood Brat and Krissy Celess, two Florida-based rappers who appear in the game.
If you want to see the full credits, including the developers and music, you don't have to beat GTA 6 to get them. Head into the Settings menu from the pause screen and tab over to Account. How to unlock GTA 6 Ultimate Edition Content and when you can use each item
The additional content you unlock through pre-ordering GTA 6 or buying the Ultimate Edition is all found in different locations
Are you wondering how to unlock the GTA 6 pre-order and GTA 6 Ultimate Edition bonuses? Don't worry, I was too through my first few hours in Leonida. It turns out that Rockstar Games doesn't want you having too many cool things too quickly, which is why you'll need to progress through different sections of the game before you'll gain access to unique weapons, vehicles, mod shops, clothing stores, and more that come as part of the DLC package, no matter which of the GTA 6 versions you own.
Below you'll find a breakdown of where to get the GTA 6 pre-order bonuses for both the Standard Edition and Ultimate Edition of the game. You'll also find a detailed breakdown of where to access all of the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition exclusives, which unlock as you progress through the story.

Where to find GTA 6 Ultimate Edition content
If you purchase the more expensive GTA 6 Ultimate Edition, you'll find that Rockstar has well and truly made it worth your while. The exclusive storefronts, weapons, clothes, and vehicles aren't immediately accessible, instead they become available once you reach certain GTA 6 mission milestones:
Once you're able to leave Jason's House in Chapter 1, you'll find two exclusive vehicles waiting for you. Underneath the house you'll find an Army fatigue-tinged Dinka Enduro motorcycle in the driveway, and off to the back of the building you'll spot a Crest Kayak which can be used to explore the Leonida Keys.
Only available for use in Chapter 1, you can get an exclusive pro build for Jason's Vapid Ganado pickup truck. Open up the map and head into the Vehicles index, from there click the Mod Shops option and look for Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento. Drive the Vapid Ganado over there and the unlock can be applied inside the Pro Builds / Full Kits section of the mod shop menu.
Take Jason to any Ammu-Nation store from Chapter 1 and access the weapon locker to unlock a personalized Girardi ES9 pistol. You can do the same with Lucia from Chapter 2 onwards, unlocking a unique Klose K17 pistol. Both of these weapons are free to equip and use.
Once you're into Chapter 2 of GTA 6, you'll be able to access unique weapon variants of the powerful Morgan revolver (regardless of whether you've already purchased the weapon). Jason and Lucia have their own versions, which can be accessed from any Ammu-Nation weapon locker.

GTA 6: How The Ultimate Edition Content Works
Ever since pre-orders for GTA 6 went live and its $100 Ultimate Edition was revealed, one question has been asked more than any other: Is it worth it?
Well, if our poll ran at the time is any indication, 70% of you certainly felt that way, with the majority opting for the full GTA 6 experience over the standard version.
We’ve long known what is included in the more expensive edition, from personalised vehicles and weapons to exclusive stores and missions. But what we didn’t know was how exactly we’d access this extra stuff or when we’d be able to use it. Well, now we do, thanks to the details listed in the in-game store, which states, “Ultimate Edition bonuses are threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia’s Story, with new items uncovered behind each chapter”.
It turns out that these extras will be sprinkled throughout the story, unlocking over the course of its chapters, with some only being available to use during certain timeframes, and even permanently removing your access to them once you pass points in Jason and Lucia’s tale.
Here’s the full list of the bonuses and when you’ll be able to get your hands on each:
Jason’s Vehicles – An army fatigue-tinged Dinka Enduro motorcycle and Crest Kayak. Only available from Jason’s house for use during Chapter 1.
Ganado Retro Build – Jason’s Vapid Ganado low-riding pickup with exclusive mods. Apply at the Coastal Auto Care mod shop in Key Lento. Only available to use in Chapter 1.
Personalised Weapon Variants – Sidearms with detailed engravings for Jason’s Girardi ES9 and Lucia’s Klose K17 pistols. Found in the variants section of the weapon locker at Ammu-Nation. Jason’s is available from Chapter 1. Lucia’s is available in Chapter 2.
Morgan Revolvers – His and hers versions of the revolver with classic Vice City stylings, sourced from the Vercetti Estate. Available from any Ammu-Nation weapon locker in Chapter 2.
One-Eyed Willie’s – A mod shop in Lake Leonida specialising in off-road modifications and hand-painted art. Available from Chapter 1.
Dominator Buggy & Garage – An off-road buggy built for the terrain of Mount Kalaga and its surrounding areas, which can be stored in the Paradise Garage in Watson Bay. Available after visiting the Mud Club with Wyman in Chapter 2.
Classic Car Collection – A string of side objectives that task you with tracking down abandoned classic cars for Wyman to restore them to their former glory. Wyman will contact you on WhatUp once you’ve collected the Dominator in Chapter 2.
Rideout Customs – A mod shop exclusive to the Ultimate Edition that specialises in detailed interiors, rims, and donk stylings. Available from Chapter 1.
PTT Youngin$ Compound – A gang compound you can raid in the Southside area of Vice City, full of special items and distinct contraband. Available from Chapter 1 once you’ve explored Southside.
Stock 305 Clothing Store – A clothing store in the Stockyard area of Vice City with elevated streetwear styles available for Jason and Lucia. Available from Chapter 1.
Sara’s Unisex Salon – A hair salon in Hamlet with signature styles for both Jason and Lucia, including facial hair for Jason and makeup and nails for Lucia. Available from Chapter 1. Signature looks available from Chapter 2.
Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor – Located in Stockyard with over 50 signature tattoo styles for both Jason and Lucia, all designed by the artist collective FAILE. Available from Chapter 1.
Vice City Merch – A collection of apparel inspired by the in-game TV show character, Macca the Gator. Available from the mannequins at The Wonder Whale in Key Lento.
Vice City Style – Exclusive outfits, tattoos, and more for Jason and Lucia. Available in your wardrobe, most tattoo parlors, and at Sara’s Unisex Salon from Chapter 4.
Shitzu Squalo – A gradient pink and blue boat equipped with an explosives-laden weapons crate. Available from the Washington Beach canal dock in Chapter 4.
‘95 Grotti Cheetah – A signature sports car with a white and red retro-futuristic livery. Available from the Pegassi Residences building in Chapter 5.
So, enjoy those early Jason vehicles while you can, as once they’re gone, they’re gone forever. Most of the bonuses are available from early on, though, and remain there for you to use throughout the whole of your adventure through Leonida. Just one word of advice: even though you can visit some places, such as Stock 305 clothing, you may not have the cash early on to actually afford anything there. It’s a pricey place.
